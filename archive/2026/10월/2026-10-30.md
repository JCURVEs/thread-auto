Along with funding to help advance inclusive innovation, Meta is donating AI glasses through CNIB Come to Work program, which will put tools into the hands of people exploring employment opportunities and building their careers. The program provides  training and practical resources to help participants assess how wearable technology can assist them in securing employment, succeeding at work and pursuing their goals. People use Meta’s AI glasses for everything from listening to music, to live translation and hands-free calls. But for people who are blind or have low vision, the difference they make can be life-changing — helping with everyday tasks like reading text, identifying objects and describing the world around them, and helping people live more independently. This partnership builds on our work with blind and low vision communities around the world, including donating Ray-Ban Meta glasses to every blind veteran in America in partnership with the Blinded Veterans Association, and our work with Vision Ireland.  &#8220;The best technology is shaped with the people who will use it,&#8221; said Angela Bonfanti, President and CEO of CNIB. &#8220;People who are blind, Deafblind or have low vision bring valuable expertise about where barriers remain and how tools like AI glasses can offer greater choice, freedom and independence in everyday life.
